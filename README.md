# Fiddletown Hikes

Hiking Buddy's public trail list for Fiddletown and Amador County, California.

**Live:** https://davedellaquila.github.io/fiddletown-hikes/

Seven local trails — distances, difficulty, typical times, highlights, permits and dog rules — each with a photo and a GPX route traced along the mapped footpath and simplified to at most 150 points for inRoute. The Indian Grinding Rock GPX is trailhead waypoints only; those trails aren't in the public map data yet.

Photos are freely licensed via Wikimedia Commons; every photographer is credited on the page. Trail geometry © OpenStreetMap contributors.

This is the public storefront. Hiking Buddy's private working material lives elsewhere and is not in this repo.
